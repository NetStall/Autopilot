## Setup

- Labelled vacancies only; each variant orders them by its score. „Passt" = relevant. Binary NDCG.
- **Nur Regeln** = `RuleRankScore` (no extraction, remote level from the rules). **Kombiniert** = `RankScore` (rules +
  extraction v3, qwen3:8b), today's default sort. **Nur KI** = stored KI-Bewertung (fitscore.v1, qwen3:8b; 11 of 16
  have one, the rest rank last).
- **Rang + w × KI** = (rank + w·KI) / (1 + w); without a KI score, the rank alone.
- **(model)** rows: extraction v3 + KI-Bewertung **fitscore.v2** by that model for all 16 labelled vacancies
  (`POST /api/evaluation/model-run`, cache only — no vacancy changed). Their „Kombiniert" also covers the 5
  labelled vacancies that had no extraction yet (unreachable ones are never in the Top N).

## Findings

1. **The extraction helps.** Kombiniert beats Nur Regeln (P@10 0.90 vs. 0.70) after the 07c fix: „Deutsch (C1)" in
   the extracted must-haves was counted a second time as a skill gap (Meierhofer 79 → 58, now 73), and MCA GROUP got
   a German level from a posting that never mentions German (now ignored).
2. **Mixing in the KI score gains nothing.** Every weight gives the same or worse numbers than Kombiniert.
3. **KI-Bewertung v1 did not see location or employer type** (MCA GROUP 75, blockers „WPF", „Azure DevOps" — both not
   required). **v2** checks location, employer type, German and must-haves first, gets the remote flag and the
   job board's home-office field, and its caps are enforced in code (qwen3:8b named „nicht erreichbar: Gronau" but
   kept 80). Now MCA GROUP gets „Dienstleister (Einsatz beim Kunden)" and 60 (qwen3:8b) or also „nicht erreichbar:
   München" and 40 (qwen3.5:9b). Dimmel gets „nicht erreichbar: Bautzen" only from qwen3.5:9b; qwen3:8b calls it a
   service provider instead.
4. **The model's location check is noisy.** qwen3:8b flagged Straubing (DPS, in my region) as not reachable and missed
   München and Bautzen. The rules decide reachability reliably; the KI score should not be trusted for it.
5. **qwen3.5:9b is not better here.** It extracts more strictly (Meierhofer: „Azure DevOps & SQL Server fehlen", rank
   43), so its Kombiniert drops to 0.70. It is about 1.6× slower, its KI scores bunch at 40–60, and it names location
   and employer blockers more consistently.
6. **My labels and the reachability rule disagree.** MCA GROUP (München, hybrid, share unknown) and Dimmel (Bautzen,
   „teilweise im Homeoffice", hidden as unreachable) are labelled „Passt". Either the label means „would apply
   anyway", or the 60 % reachability threshold is too strict for these. This needs a decision before more tuning.

## Recommendation (nothing changed — waiting for confirmation)

- Keep **Kombiniert** as the default sort and **qwen3:8b** for extraction and KI-Bewertung.
- Do **not** add the KI score to the rank. Keep it as a second opinion next to the rank and for its blockers in
  „Warum?".
- Keep **fitscore.v2** (it names location and employer type as asked). KI-Analyse re-scores the Top 100 once (~2 s each).
- Label **15 more** vacancies, ideally from the middle of the list (ranks 30–60) where the variants disagree, then
  run the evaluation again. Decide finding 6 first.
